About Malek Damiri

I’m Malek Damiri, Head of Revenue Operations, based in Atlanta, Georgia.

My work sits where finance, sales, and data meet: revenue reconciliation, executive reporting, and the data infrastructure behind both. Most of what ends up in my notes started as a number that didn’t add up the first time.

Outside work, I run (half marathons included) and hike in North Georgia when I can get out of the city. I’m a football fan. I make espresso at home, collect records, and go to shows around Atlanta; The Strokes and Fontaines D.C. are favorites. Ben, my goldendoodle, comes along whenever he’s allowed.

The longer writing, on revenue metrics, reconciliation, and dashboards people trust, is at malekdamiri.com. LinkedIn is the best way to reach me.
